## Short description

A private, gesture-driven notes app built around one Pill.

## Description

mi. Notes is a notes app that stays out of your way. Your notes sit in folders on one calm page, with a Pill at the bottom: tap + to write, hold it to search, swipe it for Settings or Recently Deleted.

• Real formatting: titles, headings, bold, colours, highlights and alignment, on one small panel.
• Bullets, numbers and checklists that carry on when you press Enter.
• Scan text from a photo or a picture, lists and all, read on your phone.
• Folders, pins and colours; hold a note to drag it where you want it.
• Undo after every delete, and Recently Deleted for 30 days.
• One-file backups and Markdown export, so your notes go wherever you do.
• Material 3 Expressive design, coloured by your wallpaper or your choice of accent.

No accounts, no tracking.
