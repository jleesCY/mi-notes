Notes that stay out of your way.

A simple, private notes app built around one Pill. Your notes sit in folders on one calm page; swipe the Pill to search them, find Settings or Recently Deleted, and tap + to start writing. Real formatting, lists that behave, and everything kept on your phone in a format you can back up and take anywhere.

Download APKView on GitHub

Gesture First

One Pill, a few swipes, nothing in the way of your notes.

Material You

Colors from your accent or your wallpaper.

Private by Default

No accounts, no tracking. Your notes never leave your phone unless you share them.

Writing

Real formatting. One small Pill.

In a note, the Pill becomes your toolbar: text styles, bold, checklists, bullets and numbers are a tap away, and it docks above the keyboard while you type. Swipe it right to undo, left to redo, and up to go back to your notes.

  • Swipe right to undo, left to redo
  • Title, Heading and Subheading styles
  • Docks above the keyboard
  • Saves as you type
Formatting

Everything on one panel.

Tap Aa for the whole toolbox: styles, bold, italic, underline and strikethrough, any text size, text colours and highlights, alignment, indents and lists. It slides up where the keyboard was, and every change shows as you make it.

  • Any text size you type
  • Text colours and highlights
  • Left, centre and right alignment
  • Six levels of indent
Structure

Lists that behave.

Bullets, numbers and checklists carry on with Enter and end on an empty line, like every editor you know. The + adds dividers, quotes, code blocks and callouts, or scans a list off paper: boxes come in as a checklist, dots as bullets, numbers as a numbered list, read on your phone.

  • Press Enter twice to end any list or block
  • Tap a checkbox to tick it off
  • Scan text from a photo or a picture
  • Exports to Markdown, and back
Organization

Hold a note. Put it anywhere.

Pick a folder from its chip at the top. Hold a note to pick it up and drag it where you want it, or let go without moving for its menu: pin it, share it, move it to a folder, rename it, give it a colour, export it or duplicate it.

  • Folders with a chip each
  • Pins, colours and manual order
  • Share as text or Markdown
  • Undo after every delete
Aesthetic

Deeply, truly yours.

Pick an accent (or use your wallpaper) and the whole Material 3 colour scheme follows. Choose staggered cards, a grid or a list; square, soft or round corners; the spacing, the font and the size of your notes' text.

  • Material You dynamic colour
  • Staggered, Grid or List layouts
  • Rounded Google Sans Flex option
  • Pure Black for OLED screens
Your Data

Your notes, your files.

Notes stay on your phone, in a plain format. Back everything up to one file, export every note as Markdown for any other notes app, or bring Markdown and text files in. Nothing is locked away.

One-file backups Markdown export Markdown & text import Share as text Share as Markdown Send text from any app
