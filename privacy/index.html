Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

mi. Notes collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no crash reporters. Everything you write stays on your device.

What stays on your device

Everything you write: your notes, folders, pictures and settings. They are stored in the app's private storage on your phone and nowhere else. You can delete them at any time with Settings → Backup & Reset → Wipe All Data, or by uninstalling mi. Notes.

They leave your phone only when you choose to: sharing a note, exporting a backup or Markdown files, or copying text. Exports are saved only where you choose to save them.

Connections mi. makes

One, to GitHub (api.github.com):

  • To check whether a newer version of mi. Notes is available, at most every 12 hours, and when you tap Check for Updates in Settings → About. It sends nothing about you or your notes; GitHub sees an ordinary request for the latest release.
  • To download the new version, if you choose Install.
  • After an update, to fetch that version's release notes for What's New.

GitHub receives your IP address and basic device information, as with any web request, under its own privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Internet: only for the update check, the update download and the release notes above.
  • Install apps: asked by Android the first time you install an update from inside mi. Notes.

Children

mi. Notes collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.
