The Complete User Guide

Every gesture, tool and setting in mi. Notes.

The Pill

mi. Notes is made for one hand. Your notes fill the page, and everything else is in the Pill at the bottom of the screen.

New Note

Tap + in the middle of the Pill. The note opens with the keyboard up, in the folder you're looking at.

Search

Tap the magnifier, or hold the Pill: it widens into a search field and your notes narrow as you type.

The ⋮ Menu

New Checklist, New Folder, Scan with Camera and Scan from Files (the text becomes a new note), and the view: Layout (Staggered, Grid or List) and Sort By (Manual, Edited, Created or Title).

Swipe Right

Recently Deleted comes in from the left with your finger. Let go past the tick to open it.

Swipe Left

Settings comes in from the right. Inside a section, swipe its Pill right to go up a level.

In a Note

Swipe Right

Undo. Keep swiping to undo further.

Swipe Left

Redo what you undid.

Swipe Up

Back to your notes. The note is already saved.

Pro Tip: Settings › About › Gesture Walkthrough lets you try every swipe again, step by step.

Writing

A note is one text, and its title is simply the first line: leave that line blank and the note has no title. The keyboard opens when you tap the text (selecting doesn't open it), and putting it away lets go of the text. In a note the Pill becomes your toolbar, and while you type it docks above the keyboard. Everything is saved as you go: there's no Save button.

Aa

The formatting panel: styles, sizes, colours and more.

Bold

Bold the selection, or what you type next.

Checklist

Turn the line into a checklist item.

Bullets

Turn the line into a bulleted list.

Numbers

Turn the line into a numbered list.

Insert

Add a divider, quote, code block or callout, or scan text in. Closing it brings the keyboard back.

At the Top: the note's folder, when it was edited and how many words it has. The pin pins it; ⋮ puts the keyboard away and opens the same menu as holding the note on the home page.

Formatting

Tap Aa and the panel slides up where the keyboard was. Select text first to change it, or set a style and keep typing.

Styles

Title, Heading, Subheading and Body set the size and weight of the whole paragraph.

Bold, Italic, Underline, Strikethrough

One tap each. Clear Formatting takes every inline style off the selection.

Text Size

Step it with − and +, or tap the number and type any size you like.

Text Color and Highlight

Pick a colour for the letters or for behind them, or None to take it off.

Alignment and Indent

Left, centre or right; indent a list item in or out, up to six levels.

Pro Tip: your selection stays while the panel is open, so you can make it bold, bigger and yellow in one go. Hold a word to select something new, and drag its handles to take in more.

Lists & Blocks

Lists and blocks behave the way they do in every other editor.

Enter Carries On

Enter starts the next item of a list, or the next line of a quote, code block or callout.

↵↵

Enter Twice Ends It

Press Enter on an empty item and the list (or block) ends there; you're back to plain text.

Backspace at the Start

Takes the bullet, number, checkbox or block off the line, keeping its text.

Checklists

Tap a checkbox to tick it off. With Checked Items to Bottom on (Settings › Notes), ticked items move below the rest.

Divider

A line across the page, between two parts of a note.

Quote

Sets a paragraph apart with a bar at its side.

Code

Monospaced, on a tinted block, with no autocorrect or capital letters getting in the way.

Callout

A tinted note with an accent bar, for the line you mustn't miss.

Scan Text

Scan with Camera takes a photo with your camera app; Scan from Files picks a picture. Its text goes in at the cursor, read on your phone: nothing is sent anywhere. Lists come in as the page has them, read from the marks themselves: boxes as a checklist (ticked ones ticked), dots and dashes as bullets, 1. and 2) as numbers, and indents as levels. A list with no marks at all comes in as bullets. Headings stay headings, two columns are read one after the other, and a table's rows come in as lines, their cells split by |. Shadows, a tilted photo and light writing on a dark board are fine. From the home page's ⋮ menu, a scan becomes a new note, its heading the title.

Organizing Notes

Folders sit as chips across the top of the home page. Pinned notes always come first.

Folders

Tap a chip to see that folder, or All Notes for everything. Make one from + at the end of the chips or the ⋮ menu; hold a chip to rename or delete it (its notes move to No Folder, and Undo puts it all back).

Hold a Note

Hold a note to pick it up, then drag it somewhere else (Sort By Manual keeps your order). Let go without moving for its menu.

Pin, Share, Move, Rename

The big buttons at the top of a note's menu. Share sends it as plain text; Move puts it in another folder.

Colour

Give the note's card one of nine colours, or none.

Tools

Markdown shares the note as a .md file; Export saves it as one; Duplicate makes a copy; Info shows when it was made and edited and how long it is. The copy button copies its text.

Swipe Rows

In the List layout, swipe a note left to delete it or right to pin it (Settings › Gestures).

Undo

Every delete shows Undo for a few seconds, and the note waits in Recently Deleted after that.

Search

Hold the Pill (or tap the magnifier) and start typing. Titles and text are both searched, in the folder you're looking at, and the list narrows with every letter.

Open a Result

Tap a note to open it. Come back and your search is still there, so you can open the next one.

No Matches

The field gives a little shake when nothing matches.

Long Press to Search

Settings › Gestures turns holding the Pill on or off. The magnifier always works.

Recently Deleted

Swipe the Pill right. Notes you delete wait here for 30 days (or a week, or 90 days: Settings › Recently Deleted), then they're gone for good.

Restore

Tap a note, or its restore button, to put it back where it was.

Delete Forever

Swipe a note left. You're asked first: this one can't be undone.

Search

The Pill here searches your deleted notes; its ⋮ empties Recently Deleted.

Your Data

Your notes live in the app's private storage on your phone, in a plain format. These are the ways in and out.

Backups

Settings › Backup & Reset › Back Up saves everything to one .minotesdata file. Restore on a new phone brings it all back; notes you already have stay.

Markdown

Export as Markdown gives you every note as a .md file in a zip. Lists, checklists, bold, italics, dividers, quotes, code and callouts (as GitHub's > [!NOTE]) come out as Markdown, and come back the same way with Import Notes.

Share a Note

Share sends a note as plain text; Markdown sends it as a .md file. Export saves that file wherever you choose.

Send Text to mi. Notes

In any app, share some text and choose mi. Notes: it becomes a new note.

New Note Shortcut

Hold the mi. Notes icon on your home screen for New Note, or drag it out as an icon of its own.

Automation Links

Apps like Tasker can open minotes://new?title=…&text=… for a new note, or minotes://append?title=…&text=… to add lines to the end of the note with that title (a new one if there's none). The note opens so you see what was added. Links only add: they can't change or delete anything, and web pages can't open them.

Copy

The copy button in a note's menu copies its text.

Updates

mi. Notes checks GitHub for a new version and offers to install it. After an update it shows What's New.

Privacy by Design

mi. Notes is minimal in design and in data collection. Your notes remain yours.

No Tracking

No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no crash reporters. The only connection is to GitHub, to check for a newer version.

Local Storage

Notes, folders and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. They leave it only when you share, copy or export them.
