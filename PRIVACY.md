# mi. Notes privacy policy

mi. Notes collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no crash reporters.

## What stays on your device

Everything you write: your notes, folders, pictures and settings. They're stored in the app's private storage on your
phone and nowhere else. They leave your phone only when you choose to: sharing a note, exporting a backup or Markdown
files, or copying text.

Scanning text reads the picture on your phone; neither the picture nor its text is sent anywhere. A photo taken with
your camera app for a scan is deleted as soon as its text has been read.

## Connections mi. makes

One, to GitHub, to check whether a newer version of mi. Notes is available (at most every 12 hours, and when you tap
Check for Updates in Settings › About). It sends nothing about you or your notes; GitHub sees an ordinary request for
the latest release. If you choose Install, the new version is downloaded from GitHub. After an update, mi. Notes asks
GitHub for that version's release notes to show you What's New.

## Permissions

- **Internet:** only for the update check, the update download and the release notes above.
- **Install apps:** asked by Android the first time you install an update from inside mi. Notes.

## Children

mi. Notes collects nothing from anyone, children included.

## Changes and contact

Any change to this policy will be published here, in the app's repository. Questions or problems:
[open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/mi-app-hub/mi-notes/issues/new).
